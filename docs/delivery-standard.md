# Afterword delivery standard

This is the operator checklist for every monthly campaign. A delivery does not leave review until every applicable item is complete.

## 1. Intake is complete

- One source recording or transcript, no longer than 75 minutes unless separately scoped.
- Customer website, audience, offer, and desired next action are recorded.
- Brand files or a documented fallback profile are present.
- One customer decision-maker is named for the consolidated review.

## 2. Editorial direction is defensible

- The guide has one central argument, stated in one sentence.
- The argument is useful to the target audience and supports the customer offer without becoming a disguised sales brochure.
- Every quote, number, factual claim, and attributed opinion can be traced to the supplied source.
- Any outside fact is flagged for customer confirmation before review.
- The copy does not invent customer experience, results, clients, or opinions.

## 3. The campaign works as one system

- The guide, eight LinkedIn posts, three emails, and landing-page copy share the same argument and call to action.
- Each social post still works as a standalone piece rather than an excerpt.
- The emails each have one job: deliver, develop, or invite.
- The landing-page blocks are labelled and can be placed without another briefing.
- Repetition across pieces is deliberate, not accidental.

## 4. Voice and brand are consistent

- Wording matches approved examples and the current brand profile.
- Customer terminology is used consistently across all thirteen pieces.
- Visual hierarchy, colour contrast, logo treatment, spacing, and page breaks have been checked.
- No Afterword branding competes with the customer brand inside customer-facing assets.

## 5. Review is frictionless

- One private review link contains the full campaign.
- Source references are visible beside the relevant material.
- The customer is told to consolidate factual, terminology, and preference changes into one round.
- A terminology or fact correction is applied across every affected asset.
- Review decisions are added to the reusable brand profile.

## 6. Final delivery is complete

- PDF renders without clipping, overflow, missing glyphs, or blank pages.
- Links work and filenames are clear.
- LinkedIn posts and emails are supplied in editable text formats.
- The source-reference map and campaign JSON are included for auditability.
- The final ZIP opens successfully and contains only the current approved version.
- Delivery message lists the contents and the recommended publishing order.

## Stop conditions

Do not deliver if a source-backed claim cannot be verified, the PDF layout check fails, required pieces are missing, or the customer has not approved a material fact introduced during review.
